01

The short first month

Starting after the 1st cuts gross by calendar days. Tax credits for that month often stay whole, so net is not a simple fraction of a full month.

02

Three statutory slices

PAYE is income tax after credits. USC is separate and ignores occupational pension. PRSI is social insurance — 4.2% until October 2026, then 4.35%.

03

Pension is a fork

A workplace scheme usually means tax relief at 40% on this salary. No scheme means MyFutureFund at 1.5%, taken from net pay.
